**This content is in English.** Launching a product is exciting, but getting the email campaign right can make or break your launch. We've analyzed hundreds of product launch emails to bring you six outstanding examples that convert browsers into buyers.

The best product launch emails have a few things in common: they create urgency, highlight benefits clearly, and make the call-to-action impossible to miss. But most importantly, they tell a story that resonates with their audience's needs and desires.

<Blockquote name="Marketing Expert">
  A great launch email doesn't just announce a product—it invites customers into a story where they're the hero and your product is the tool that helps them succeed.
</Blockquote>

From Apple's minimalist approach to Dropbox's playful tone, each example demonstrates different strategies for engaging audiences. The common thread? They all understand their customers deeply and craft messages that speak directly to their pain points and aspirations.

Whether you're launching a SaaS product, a physical good, or a service, these principles apply. Study what works, adapt it to your brand's voice, and always put your customer at the center of the story.
